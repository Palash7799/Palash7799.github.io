Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Open How to Play from the main menu. The tutorial takes about a minute and covers the complete ruleset.

The short version: on your turn you either move your pawn one square, or place a wall to block a path. You may never wall an opponent in completely — there must always be a route to their goal. First pawn to reach the opposite side wins.

I bought Wallfare Pro but it isn't showing

Open Store from the main menu and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to the store account you bought with — your Apple Account on iPhone or iPad, your Google Account on Android — so make sure you're signed in with the same one. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

How do hints and undos work?

You get 5 hints and 5 undos free every day, topped back up automatically.

Run out? Watch a short ad for one more, as many times as you like — there is no daily limit, so you never have to stop playing. If you would rather not watch ads, you can buy a pack of 25, or unlock Wallfare Pro once for unlimited hints and undos with no ads at all.

Undo is available in single-player and pass-and-play games. It's disabled in online matches, so neither player can rewind a move the other has already seen.

What is the Daily Challenge?

One board per day, generated from the date, so every player in the world faces exactly the same layout against the Hard CPU. Win it and your streak grows. Miss a day and the streak resets. The board changes at midnight, local time.

My online match won't connect

  • Check that both players typed the same room code
  • Room codes expire once a match ends — create a fresh one for each game
  • Some restricted networks (office or school Wi-Fi) block the connection; try mobile data instead

If a match drops mid-game, reopening the app and rejoining with the same code usually restores it. It can take up to 30 seconds for the server to notice the old connection has gone, so if it says the room is full, wait a moment and try again.

Does Wallfare work offline?

Yes. Everything except Online mode works with no internet connection at all.

What data does the game collect?

Wallfare has no accounts and asks for no personal information. Online matches relay only board moves and a temporary room code, and nothing is kept once a match ends.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from, so refunds go through them rather than us.

On iPhone or iPad, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history. Sign in, find Wallfare, and select the purchase.

I found a bug / I have an idea

Please tell us — email above. Small studios rely on this, and a good bug report genuinely does turn into a fix.
